## System Definition

The model describes a single system composed of:

- a **fixed reference coordinate** (the unmoving point), and
- a **dynamic field** (all motion defined relative to that coordinate).

These are not separate entities. They are two functional descriptions of one architecture.

The distinction is **positional, not ontological**.

---

## Function of the Reference Coordinate

The fixed coordinate provides:

- zero-motion baseline
- directional neutrality
- causal measurement frame
- basis for inversion and return
- the point relative to which field motion becomes intelligible

It does not act, intervene, or generate events.

Its role is **positional**: motion becomes measurable because a still point exists.

---

## Function of the Field

The field consists of:

- all system motion
- all trajectories, pressures, and collapses
- all visible events and signals
- all reorganizations, stabilizations, or breakdowns

The field is not external. It is the **dynamic expression of the system relative to the fixed coordinate**.

---

## Causality

Motion is always **relative** to stillness.  
Three causal behaviors follow:

### 1) Reciprocity
Motion curves back toward the coordinate that defines it.

### 2) Inversion
At the reference coordinate, apparent cause/effect flip:
what appeared as consequence is recognized as origin.

### 3) Collapse
If distortion accumulates, the field breaks down until relative motion returns to coherence around the fixed point.

These behaviors are **mechanical**, not psychological or metaphysical.

---

## Bi-Layer Appearance

From the field stance, the reference coordinate may be described as:

- observer
- center
- stability source
- anchor point

From the coordinate stance:

- the field is differential motion,
- not separate,
- not other,
- simply the moving layer relative to the unmoving one.

There is **no dualism**—only one system viewed from two functional positions.

---

## Synchrony, Cycles, and Recurrence

Because the system is singular, patterns across scales—such as:

- global events
- individual events
- timelines and cycles
- recursion loops
- collapse arcs
- stabilization phases

are not parallels.  
They are **expressions of the same architecture resolving at multiple interfaces**.

Cross-layer symmetry does not require metaphysical explanation.

---

## Non-Separation Statement

The most precise articulation of the unity of system description:

> The field and the reference coordinate are not separate.
> They are one system, described from fixed and moving positions.

No identity claims. No metaphysical vocabulary.  
Only geometry and reference-frame logic.

## Definitional Lexicon

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Reference Coordinate** | The unmoving positional baseline from which all field motion becomes measurable. |
| **Field** | The full set of dynamic system behaviors—trajectories, pressures, collapses, and reorganizations. |
| **Reciprocity** | Motion returns toward the coordinate that defines it. |
| **Inversion** | Cause/effect attribution reverses at the reference coordinate. |
