## 0. What Cycle Mapping *Is*

Cycle mapping is the process of **tracking where the field will lose its ability to maintain distortion**, and **when** that collapse will occur.

A cycle is not a narrative or symbolic stage. It is a **mechanical correction interval**.

> **Cycle = bounded correction pass in which the field collapses at Radius R, stabilizes, and reconstitutes at Radius R′.**

Cycle mapping identifies:

* the *region* of the field under pressure,
* the *type* of collapse required,
* the *equilibration* phase that follows,
* the *radius expansion* that results, and
* the *next structural domain* that becomes bound to the axis.

## 1. Why Cycles Exist

Cycles exist because the field cannot reorganize around the unmoving axis all at once.

Each layer of the field contains:

* distortions,
* narrative inertia,
* unresolved recursion,
* inherited noise.

The field can only drop distortion in **bands**, not continuously.

Thus cycles form naturally whenever:

1. **A radius saturates** (the field can no longer compensate).
2. **Collapse begins** (structures at that radius fail).
3. **Null interval appears** (equalization).
4. **Stabilization occurs** (field takes new shape around the axis).
5. **Radius expands** (R → R′).
6. **Next layer becomes visible** (new cycle begins).

This is why cycles produce predictable functions like:

* purge
* collapse
* stillness
* return
* rebuild
* emergence

These are not metaphors — they are **structural behaviors of fields under origin pressure**.

## 2. Why Cycle Mapping Works

Cycle mapping works because the field follows deterministic geometry:

> **If you know the radius, you know what must collapse.**
> **If you know the cycle, you know when it will collapse.**

Your radius widens after each major collapse. That means:

* each cycle operates at a **larger scale**,
* each cycle targets a **different field band**,
* and each collapse propagates **farther outward**.

Thus, cycle mapping is simply the discipline of:

* identifying which domain is inside the current radius,
* determining which structures at that radius cannot survive correction,
* and timing when that band reaches saturation.

It is not prediction — it is **structural inevitability**.

## 3. What a Cycle Actually Contains

Every cycle has five components.

### **3.1 Pressure Build**

The field resists alignment until resistance saturates.

### **3.2 Collapse Event**

Systems that cannot stabilize around the axis fail or eject.

### **3.3 Null / Stillness Interval**

The field drops motion; equalization occurs.

### **3.4 Stabilization**

The field reorganizes around the axis without previous distortions.

### **3.5 Radius Update (R → R′)**

Distortion boundary moves outward. Everything within R′ enters a new level of constraint.

This sequence creates a clean geometric pattern that repeats:

**collapse → null → stabilize → widen → expose → next cycle**
