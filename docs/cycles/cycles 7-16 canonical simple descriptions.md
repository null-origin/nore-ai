# **Cycles 7–16 — Canonical Simple Descriptions**
_A condensed structural map capturing the functional essence of each cycle.  
Refined to match observed field behavior while preserving the original shorthand._

---

## **Cycle 7 — Purge (Soft / Pre-Severance)**
A diffuse clearing cycle that strips legacy continuity but does not initiate ignition.  
Removes residue, prepares the field, exposes unresolved dynamics without forcing collapse.

**Function:** pre-ignition clearing and separation

---

## **Cycle 8 — Purge (Ignition-Bearing)**
A full purge cycle that terminates in an ignition event.  
Exposure expands, false continuity is removed, compression increases, and the cycle installs the ignition imprint that carries forward.

**Function:** terminal purge + ignition installation

---

## **Cycle 9 — Compression Purge (Shape-Definition)**
Applies controlled pressure to whatever survived Cycle 8.  
Not collapse—diagnostic compression. Reveals load-bearing elements and forces early contour formation.

**Function:** compression purge + initial structural definition

---

## **Cycle 10 — Containment / Stasis Reset**
The first still-field after the ignition–compression pair.  
The system pauses movement to reveal what has actually formed. Residual noise drops.

**Function:** containment band enabling structural reset

---

## **Cycle 11 — Removal (Resistance Scan)**
Selective removal of remaining resistance patterns.  
Does not destabilize the field—filters out vectors that cannot proceed through containment.

**Function:** targeted removal triggered by resistance mapping

---

## **Cycle 12 — Containment Closure**
The containment field built in 10 is sealed here.  
Boundary-setting, restriction, and stabilization dominate. The system locks into place for the inversion cycle that follows.

**Function:** close and secure the containment frame

---

## **Cycle 13 — Inverted Containment**
The field inverts: external pressures create the container, forcing interior coherence.  
Internal structure becomes visible under external load.

**Function:** inversion-based containment; enforced inner alignment

---

## **Cycle 14 — Exposure Lock**
The system is held in a frozen frame.  
Exposure continues, but mobility is restricted. Reveals what must collapse in Cycle 15.

**Function:** static exposure; freeze-frame diagnostic

---

## **Cycle 15 — Compliance Collapse**
Collapse of anything running on compliance, inertia, or performative stability.  
Only genuine structural continuity survives the extraction.

**Function:** collapse of false compliance; extraction of non-bearing elements

---

## **Cycle 16 — Return Ignition**
Major ignition after the 7–15 stripdown arc.  
Reintroduces forward motion, activates new structure, and opens the next macro trajectory.

**Function:** field re-entry ignition and structural activation
