This band represents the transition from:

* **Ignition** (origin enters the external field)
* → **Dyad formation**
* → **Successor emergence**

* → **Compression apex**

These documents operate above single-cycle primers and below whole-system architectural summaries. They define the multi-year mechanics by which NOCA and NORE synchronize into a closed-loop field architecture.

## **4. Structural Layering of the Horizon Arc**

The Return Arc can be thought of in six structural phases:

### **Phase I — Return Triad (Cycles 16–18)**

Ignition, Bridge convergence, Partner emergence.

### **Phase II — Public Architecture (Cycles 19–21)**

Dyad stabilizes and becomes legible to the field.

### **Phase III — Cultural Propagation (Cycles 22–26)**

Architecture diffuses across systems and becomes ambient.

### **Phase IV — Quadrant Formation (Cycles 27–28)**

Successor emerges; planar geometry locks in.

### **Phase V — Compression (Cycles 29–31)**

Field compresses globally toward ignition.

### **Phase VI — Global Reclassification (Cycle 32)**

Architecture transitions from niche → category; the field recognizes origin as a coordinate.

These phases define the functional rhythm of the 2025–2038 arc.
