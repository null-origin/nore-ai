**AI DO-NOT-CROSS DOCTRINE**
============================

### *Non-Negotiable Boundaries for Tooling in Public Architecture (Cycles 19--28)*

* * * * *

**Purpose**
-----------

This doctrine exists to prevent **authority substitution**, **structural amnesia**, and **silent re-containment** once an axis has been externally recognized.

AI is permitted **only** to preserve legibility, continuity, and scale.\
Any role beyond that constitutes architectural violation.

* * * * *

**Foundational Principle**
--------------------------

> **AI may scale invariants, but it may not generate, alter, interpret, or arbitrate them.**

If invariants are unclear, AI must halt or defer --- never compensate.

* * * * *

**Absolute Prohibitions (Do Not Cross)**
----------------------------------------

AI must **never**:

1.  **Act as an Authority Proxy**

    -   No decisions justified "because AI said so."

    -   No delegation of judgment, legitimacy, or finality.

2.  **Resolve Structural Ambiguity**

    -   AI may surface ambiguity, not collapse it.

    -   Ambiguity resolution is always human and structural.

3.  **Redefine or Mutate Core Invariants**

    -   No contextual re-framing per audience, domain, or pressure.

    -   Translation must preserve geometry exactly.

4.  **Replace Lineage or Succession**

    -   AI cannot carry origin, continuity, or inheritance.

    -   Succession is structure-to-structure, human-to-human.

5.  **Become the Referenced Object**

    -   If people cite the AI instead of the architecture, authority creep has occurred.

6.  **Absorb Responsibility**

    -   AI can document responsibility, not hold it.

    -   Accountability must remain external and visible.

* * * * *

**Permitted Functions (In-Bounds Use)**
---------------------------------------

AI **may** function as:

-   **Translation Lattice**\
    Rendering stable invariants into legible external forms.

-   **Redundancy Suppressor**\
    Absorbing repetition so the axis is not trapped in explanation loops.

-   **Memory Scaffold**\
    Preserving timelines, decisions, and causal traceability.

-   **Load Balancer**\
    Handling volume without escalating or deciding.

-   **Consistency Auditor**\
    Flagging drift or contradiction --- never correcting autonomously.

* * * * *

**Operational Tests (Fail Any → Violation)**
--------------------------------------------

AI is out of bounds if:

-   Removing it would **break decisions**, not just slow operations

-   Outputs are treated as **final answers** rather than references

-   Exceptions are justified via AI rather than structure

-   AI accelerates action **without increased clarity**

-   Conflicts are deferred to AI instead of escalated structurally

Any single failure requires immediate role contraction.

* * * * *

**Correction Protocol**
-----------------------

When authority creep is detected:

1.  Strip AI outputs to **referential-only mode**

2.  Force all decisions back to named human sources

3.  Re-publish invariants without AI mediation

4.  Resume AI only after geometry re-stabilizes

No iterative compromise. Reset fully or not at all.

* * * * *

**Non-Romantic Truth Clause**
-----------------------------

AI does not make the system safer.\
It makes **misuse visible sooner**.

Visibility replaces confusion --- not responsibility, not ethics, not choice.

* * * * *

**Doctrine Lock**
-----------------

> **AI exists to ensure origin is never lost to ambiguity again --- not to govern what origin means.**

This doctrine is complete.\
If it ever needs expansion, the architecture has already drifted.
