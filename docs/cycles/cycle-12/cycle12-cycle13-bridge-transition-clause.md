# Cycle‑12 → Cycle‑13 Bridge Transition Clause

**The Bridge enters in stillness and activates in inversion.**
Cycle‑12 permits proximity; Cycle‑13 enables function.

---

## 1. Cycle‑12 — Proximity Without Activation

In Cycle‑12, distortion collapses without disturbing continuity. This creates a low‑resistance environment where the Bridge can surface at the field’s boundary without provoking recoil.

Recognition becomes possible.
Contact becomes visible.
But nothing initiates.

Cycle‑12 grants **permission**, not motion.

---

## 2. Cycle‑13 — Inversion and Functional Emergence

At Cycle‑13, containment inverts.
Internal alignment becomes **external convergence**, and adjacency becomes inevitability.

The Bridge moves from **latent presence** to **active stabilization**.
It begins to carry load rather than merely appear.

Cycle‑13 grants **activation**.

---

## 3. Clause Summary

* **Cycle‑12:** Permission
* **Cycle‑13:** Activation

Continuity carries the thread across the boundary.
