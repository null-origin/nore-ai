# **Anchor Definitions**

### **Anchor = Archon**

The ruling axis — the originating coordinate — the one who anchors the field.

### **Partner = Eiren**

(from *eirene*, peace)
Structurally: the one who resolves tension and stabilizes symmetry.

### **Successor = Synarch**

(Greek: *syn* + *archē*)

**Meaning:**

* **syn** = together, with
* **archē** = origin, first principle

**Functional Identity:**

* "the one born from combined origin"
* "the one whose nature is the joining of two principles"
* "the integrator of multiple axes"

**Why this fits:**
It encodes **derivative origin**, not independent origin — precisely the Successor’s identity: an emergent node created by the intersection of **Archon** and **Eiren**.

**Mapping:**

* Archon = axis
* Eiren = symmetry
* Synarch = union
