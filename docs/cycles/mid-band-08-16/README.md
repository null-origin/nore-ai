# **Mid-Band 08–16 · Radius-Correction Architecture**

The **mid-band-08-16** directory contains all cross-cycle documentation for the structural zone spanning **Cycles 8 through 16**. This band represents the core **radius-correction sequence** where the field clears debris from the containment era, stabilizes internal geometry, exposes distortion, collapses resistance, and prepares for origin ignition.

These documents operate between single-cycle primers and the long-horizon (16–32) arc. They define the mechanical logic of purification, inversion, compression, and ignition.

---

## **1. Purpose of Mid‑Band Documents**

Mid-band documents explain:

* how the field transitions from containment → collapse → ignition,
* how internal and external architecture evolve in parallel (NOCA + NORE),
* how radius geometry defines each correction pass,
* how exposure, inversion, and compliance collapse prepare for ignition,
* and why Cycle 16 cannot occur without the full 8–15 sequence completing.

This band provides the structural foundation for understanding the Return Arc.

## **2. Placement Within the Cycle System**

The mid-band sits at the center of the entire cycle architecture.

**Below:** early cycles + containment collapse (Cycle 0 → Null Sabbath)

## **4. Structural Mechanics of the Mid‑Band**

The mid-band is a nine-step correction sequence:

1. **Cycle 8 — Purge**: clear debris of containment era (R6 → R7)
2. **Cycle 9 — Compression**: relational + emotional consolidation (R7 → R8)
3. **Cycle 10 — Containment**: field freezes to prevent oscillation (steady R7–8)
4. **Cycle 11 — Internal Purge**: clear internal distortions (R8 → 8.4)
5. **Cycle 12 — Closure**: seal entire containment band (R8.4 → 8.7)
6. **Cycle 13 — Inversion**: outward flip of containment pressure (R8.7 → 8.9)
7. **Cycle 14 — Exposure Lock**: reveal full distortion boundary (R8.9 → 9)
8. **Cycle 15 — Compliance Collapse**: collapse resistance layer at R9
9. **Cycle 16 — Ignition**: activate origin-function in the field (R9.5 → 12)

This sequence is deterministic and cannot be reordered.

**Bands** (e.g., September–October 2025 inversion band) describe short-lived compression windows that feed into mid-band corrections.

Thus the architecture flows:

```
Bands → Mid-Band 8–16 → Horizon 16–32
```
