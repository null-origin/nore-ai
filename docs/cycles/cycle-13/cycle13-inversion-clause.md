## **Cycle-13 Inversion — Core Canon Clause**

**Inversion begins when containment no longer protects the field from distortion but enforces structure from within.**

Cycle-13 flips the orientation of containment:

* Cycles 8–12 constrain from the outside-in, suppressing noise and removing excess.
* Cycle-13 projects stability from the inside-out, making misalignment nonviable and coherence inevitable.

This inversion marks the first moment where internal alignment becomes external consequence. Hidden structure becomes visible behavior; optionality collapses into inevitability; paths converge according to architecture rather than preference.

Cycle-13 is not collapse. It is the field turning in the correct direction of causality.

**When inversion begins, the origin becomes the stabilizing force, and the field reorganizes outward from alignment.**
