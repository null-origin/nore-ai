## Event Cluster

### 1. AI Memory Becomes Strategic Infrastructure

SK Hynix posted record first-quarter profit and revenue as memory prices rose under intense AI demand. HBM demand, wafer constraints, and customer procurement urgency reveal memory as a load-bearing AI chokepoint rather than a background component.

**Structural function:** Memory shifts from commodity input to strategic constraint.

### 2. International Institutions Lose Routing Authority

Justin Trudeau argued that institutions such as the WTO and IMF are no longer fit for purpose and that middle powers may need smaller “microlateral” alliances. This reflects the weakening of broad multilateral routing under great-power fragmentation.

**Structural function:** Coordination migrates from broad institutions to smaller, harder alignment blocs.

### 3. Energy Chokepoint Becomes Systemic Constraint

The IEA warned of the largest energy-security threat in history as the Strait of Hormuz closure disrupts oil, jet fuel, and vital commodities. Emergency stock releases buy time but do not resolve the underlying blockage.

**Structural function:** Physical transit chokepoints become macro-system limiters.

### 4. Cannabis Reclassification Opens a Regulatory Valve

The Trump administration moved to reclassify cannabis from Schedule I to Schedule III for certain products and state medical systems, lowering barriers to research and improving financial conditions for operators.

**Structural function:** A rigid legacy classification partially yields under accumulated mismatch.

### 5. Hormuz Escalation Hardens Control Logic

Trump ordered the Navy to “shoot and kill” boats laying mines in the Strait of Hormuz and asserted U.S. control over traffic through the waterway. The chokepoint becomes an explicit enforcement zone.

**Structural function:** Rerouting pressure hardens into militarized gatekeeping.

### 6. Meta Converts Labor Compression Into AI Acceleration

Meta announced a 10% workforce reduction and cancelled thousands of open roles while deepening AI investment. The company is shifting labor structure, internal monitoring, and operational priority toward AI agent development.

**Structural function:** Human labor is compressed as AI becomes the organizing investment axis.

### 7. Intel Re-enters as AI Infrastructure Node

Intel beat expectations, issued strong guidance, and showed growth in data-center CPUs and foundry/packaging. CPU demand is reappearing as agentic workloads expand beyond GPU-only architectures.

**Structural function:** Compute topology broadens; CPUs and packaging regain strategic relevance.

### 8. Texas Instruments Confirms Analog Layer Demand

Texas Instruments surged after strong results and guidance, driven by analog-chip demand for AI data centers. Power regulation and signal conversion become essential support layers for the AI buildout.

**Structural function:** The AI stack extends into foundational analog infrastructure.

### 9. Nike Restructures Under Automation Pressure

Nike cut 1,400 roles, mostly in technology, as part of its turnaround and automation-linked restructuring. The company is reorganizing operations around speed, manufacturing modernization, and supply-chain integration.

**Structural function:** Non-tech sectors absorb AI/automation pressure through labor and operational compression.

### 10. Israel-Lebanon Ceasefire Extension Delays Release

The Israel-Lebanon ceasefire was extended by three weeks, creating temporary diplomatic breathing room without resolving the underlying conflict geometry.

**Structural function:** Instability is deferred rather than discharged.

### 11. Prediction Markets Expose Information Leakage

A U.S. Special Forces master sergeant was arrested for allegedly using classified information to profit from Polymarket bets tied to the Maduro capture operation. Prediction markets convert privileged state knowledge into tradable exposure.

**Structural function:** Classified information leaks into financialized probability markets, forcing enforcement after exposure.
