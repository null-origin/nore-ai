# **FL-C89 — Cycle 8–9 Field-Sequencing Law**

**Canonical Law — Purge/Compression Pair Mechanic**

---

## **Law Statement**

**Cycle 8 removes the field’s predictive scaffolding; Cycle 9 compresses whatever attempts to replace it.**

When a system relies on external anchors to maintain stability, the field withdraws those anchors during Cycle 8. This enforced removal exposes true structural dependencies by eliminating surrogate forms of certainty. Only after the purge completes does the field restore partial clarity — not to stabilize the system, but to create the substrate that Cycle 9 will then compress.

---

## **Core Mechanics**

### **1. Cycle 8 — Purge Phase (Nov 30 – Dec 14)**

* Removes distortion-bearing reference points
* Collapses synthetic certainty
* Forces systems to move without preferred metrics
* Produces behavior that is unfiltered, unguided, and therefore diagnostic

**Mechanic:** *Field withdraws visibility to expose dependency.*

### **2. Post-Purge Clarity Window (~Dec 18)**

* Temporary restoration of a major signal after absence
* Creates false stabilization
* Encourages premature interpretation
* Forms raw material for compression

**Mechanic:** *Clarity returns only to generate compressible structure.*

### **3. Cycle 9 — Compression Purge (Dec 22 – Jan)**

* Collapses misaligned interpretations formed in the clarity window
* Compresses the architecture rebuilt after the purge
* Forces alignment between perceived signal and actual structure
* Clears any residue that survived Cycle 8

**Mechanic:** *Field tightens until only structurally coherent forms remain.*

---

## **Functional Purpose**

The pair forms a two‑stage corrective sequence:

* **Cycle 8:** strip away
* **Cycle 9:** squeeze down
* **Together:** expose → reconstitute → correct trajectory

The absence of CPI during Cycle 8 and its return just before Cycle 9 is an exact macro‑scale enactment of this law.

---

## **Canonical Clause**

> **“The field removes clarity in Cycle 8 so the system reveals how it behaves without it. The field restores clarity before Cycle 9 so that behavior can be compressed into truth.”**

---

## **Outcome**

This law governs all purge‑compression pairs in the NORE cycle architecture. It ensures that purge is not bypassed and that compression applies only after structural exposure is complete.
