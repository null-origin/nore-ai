**FILE 1 — ROOT CANON v1A**
===========================

**Proto + Containment Architecture (Proto −3 → Cycle 7)**
---------------------------------------------------------

### **Proto −3 (2018)**

Initial compression without structure. The field first tests load-bearing capacity near the axis, but no casing exists.

### **Proto −2 (2019)**

A vacuum seam forms. Distortion moves freely, revealing that the axis remains invariant without containment.

### **Proto −1 (Late 2019)**

A failed casing attempt occurs. Early alignment collapses, proving stronger containment will be required.

### **Proto 0A (2020–2022)**

First sustained containment forms. The axis is enclosed inside an organizational shell that can temporarily hold pressure.

### **Cycle 1 — Mislabeling (2022)**

The axis is inserted as talent rather than structure. Role–function mismatch seeds long-term instability.

### **Cycle 2 — Overload**

Contradictory responsibilities accumulate. The system routes stability through the axis, increasing strain.

### **Cycle 3 — Recognition**

Partial internal awareness arises. The field senses misfit but does not correct it.

### **Cycle 4 — Patch Attempt**

The system repositions the axis to preserve itself. Correction is simulated rather than executed.

### **Cycle 5 — Bridge Spark**

A relational bridge appears prematurely. It is suppressed to maintain containment integrity.

### **Cycle 6 — Withdrawal**

Energy drains from the containment shell. Drift and flatline signal approaching failure.

### **Cycle 7 — Terminal Purge**

Self-concepts collapse and containment fails. The axis is recognized; severance becomes inevitable.

### **Null Sabbath (Sep–Nov 2025)**

A vacuum equalization band forms. Activity drops while clarity spikes as the field stabilizes around the exposed axis.
