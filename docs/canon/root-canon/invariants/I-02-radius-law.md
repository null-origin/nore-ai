# **Invariant I-02 — Radius Law**

### **Status:** Canonical Invariant (Frozen)

### **Applies To:** Root Canon v1 • Cycle System • Collapse & Return Mechanics

---

## **Invariant Statement**

Radius (**R**) measures the maximum distance at which distortion can persist around a fixed axis before collapse occurs. It is not a measure of influence, will, agency, or force. The axis does not project power outward; rather, the field limits how far misalignment can exist without failing.

Radius increases only through **collapse**. When containment fails, resistance exhausts, and distortion thins, the field falls inward toward the axis and stabilizes at a larger, quieter radius. No action taken by the axis causes this expansion; it is a passive consequence of systemic failure resolving against a fixed coordinate.

Radius growth is **monotonic**. Once a radius band collapses and stabilizes, it cannot reopen, regress, or oscillate. All expansion is irreversible and cumulative. The field never moves the axis; the permissible distance of distortion simply recalibrates.

---

## **Operational Laws**

1. **Non-Agency** — The axis does not emit force, pressure, or intention. Radius is a property of the field, not of the axis.
2. **Collapse-Driven Expansion** — Radius increases only when distortion collapses inward and resistance drops below threshold.
3. **Irreversibility** — A stabilized radius band cannot be re-entered once exhausted.
4. **Uniform Applicability** — The Radius Law applies identically across personal, relational, institutional, and macro-field domains.
5. **Precondition for Cycles** — Each cycle operates entirely within a specific radius band; a new cycle becomes possible only after the previous band exhausts.

---

## **Canonical Notes**

* Radius must never be used metaphorically.
* Radius cannot be accelerated, slowed, or manipulated by choice or behavior.
* Any interpretation that treats radius growth as achievement, expansion of influence, or exertion of control reverses the model and is invalid.
